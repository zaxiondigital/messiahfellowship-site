Privacy Policy

We value the trust you place in us when you share a prayer request, a message or an application. This page explains, in plain words, what information our website collects, why, and how we look after it.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short

  • We only collect what you type into our forms, plus anonymous visit statistics.
  • Your details are used only to reply, to pray for you, or to process your application.
  • Prayer requests are treated as confidential and shared only with our prayer team.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your information.

1. Who we are

This website, messiahfellowship.net, belongs to Messiah Fellowship, a church at 55-1-5, 100 Feet Road, Near Autonagar Gate, Auto Nagar, Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh 520007, India. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “the church” mean Messiah Fellowship.

2. Information you give us

We only receive personal information when you choose to send it through one of our forms:

FormWhat we ask forWhy
Prayer request
(footer of every page)		Name, email, city, phone / WhatsApp number, your prayer requestTo pray for you and, if you wish, to contact you

Please share only what is needed. A prayer request may include personal matters such as health or family; we treat these with special care (see section 5).

3. Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand how our website is used, for example which pages are visited most, how visitors find us (Google search, WhatsApp, YouTube) and which towns and countries they come from. This helps us improve the website.

  • Google Analytics uses small files called cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) to count visits.
  • It records things like pages viewed, approximate location (city), device and browser type. It does not give us your name, email or phone number.
  • We keep this statistical data for up to 14 months.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. The website works normally without them.

4. How we use your information

  • To pray for you and respond to your prayer request.
  • To reply to your messages and questions.
  • To process Bible College applications.
  • To arrange ministry and volunteer opportunities you have asked about.
  • To keep the website working well and secure.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we will not add you to any mailing list unless you ask us to.

5. Prayer requests are confidential

Prayer requests are read by our pastors and prayer team only. We do not publish them or share them outside the church without your permission.

6. Who else handles your information

We never sell your personal information. We use a few trusted services to run the website:

  • Web3Forms delivers the details you submit in our forms to the church's email inbox. It may keep a copy for a short time to make sure the message is delivered.
  • Google provides our email (Gmail), Google Analytics and the website fonts.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Links to other services: our website links to YouTube (our services and live streams), Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Google Maps. When you use those links, the privacy policy of that service applies.

Online giving: if you give online, your payment is handled securely by our payment partner. The church does not receive or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login details.

7. How long we keep it

We keep messages and applications only as long as we need them for the purpose you sent them for (for example, to follow up your prayer request or complete an admission), and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

8. Keeping it safe

Our website uses a secure connection (HTTPS, the padlock in your browser), so information you send through our forms is encrypted on the way. Access to messages is limited to the people at the church who need it.

9. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, or stop using it (you may withdraw your consent at any time);
  • address any complaint or concern you have about how we handle it.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

Children under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before sending us their details. Parents who believe their child has sent us information can contact us to have it removed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add online giving or new website features. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
