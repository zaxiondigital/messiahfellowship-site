WOMEN’S MINISTRY

Women’s Ministry

The Women’s Ministry at Messiah Fellowship provides a meaningful space for women to grow in faith, build relationships, and encourage one another in their walk with God.

We meet on the second Tuesday of every month, creating a time of fellowship, prayer, and spiritual enrichment. These gatherings are designed to strengthen women through the Word of God and help them grow deeper in their relationship with Christ.

Women of all ages and backgrounds come together to share life, support one another, and learn how to apply biblical principles in everyday living. Through worship, teaching, and fellowship, the ministry nurtures spiritual growth and unity.

This ministry also focuses on practical aspects of life, helping women grow in faith, family, and purpose, while encouraging them to live confidently in God’s calling.

We seek to lead women into a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ, helping them grow spiritually and live out their faith in everyday life.

Through teaching, fellowship, and prayer, women are encouraged to strengthen their walk with God and become godly influences in their homes and communities.

At Messiah Fellowship, women from all backgrounds come together to support one another and build strong families and communities.
