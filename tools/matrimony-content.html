A free marriage introduction service at Messiah Fellowship, helping Telugu Christian families find a life partner who shares their faith in Jesus Christ.

  • Free of cost
  • Held at the church
  • Open to all castes
Marriage, God’s way

A covenant built on faith

Marriage is God’s gift and one of the most important decisions of life. The Bible describes it as a lifelong covenant between a husband and wife, founded on love, faithfulness and a shared walk with God.

When both partners share faith in Christ, their home is built on a strong foundation: praying together, growing together, and raising their children in the knowledge of the Lord.

“What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.”

Mark 10:9

Open to every Christian family

This service welcomes Telugu Christians from all castes and communities, including those who have come to faith in Christ from other backgrounds. In Christ, every believer is one family.

“For ye are all one in Christ Jesus.” Galatians 3:28

How it works

  1. 1

    Matrimony drive at the church

    The church holds matrimony drives on announced days at the Messiah Convention Center.

  2. 2

    Come with your family

    Candidates and their parents meet in person, in a respectful setting guided by the church.

  3. 3

    Free of cost

    There is no fee. This is a service of Messiah Fellowship to Christian families.
