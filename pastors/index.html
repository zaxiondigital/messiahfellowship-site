Shepherds of Messiah Fellowship

For more than five decades, God has used faithful leaders to build Messiah Fellowship, from the founding vision of Pastor Sajja Eliazer to the pastoral care of Dr. Sajja Ratna Kumar and Mrs. Sajja Jyothi.

Our Pastors

Dr. Sajja Ratna Kumar & Mrs. Sajja Jyothi

They serve Messiah Fellowship with a shared passion for building Christ-centered lives and strong families.

With a heart for the Gospel and a commitment to discipleship, they lead with wisdom, humility, and unwavering faith. Their desire is to see individuals discover their purpose in Christ and grow in spiritual maturity through prayer, teaching, and community.

Together, they continue to shepherd the church with the vision to love God, love people, and impact the world through the message of Jesus Christ.

Founder & President

Pastor Sajja Eliazer

The founder and president of Messiah Fellowship, a ministry that has faithfully served for more than five decades.

Born as Sajja Gowthameswara Rao and raised in a Hindu family in the village of Koduru, Mudinepalli Mandalam, his life took a transformative turn after his younger son, Ratna Kumar, was miraculously healed from a life-threatening illness. Through this powerful encounter, he came to know Jesus Christ personally and surrendered his life to the Gospel.

For over 50 years, Pastor Eliazer has been in full-time ministry, passionately proclaiming the saving and miracle-working power of Jesus Christ across Andhra Pradesh and beyond. Known for his bold preaching and unwavering faith, he has become a respected and influential voice in Christian ministry throughout the region.

  • 50+ yearsin full-time ministry
  • Mahima Gadiyaluone of the most-watched Christian television programs
  • Messiah Convention Center100 Feet Road, Autonagar Gate, Vijayawada

His television program, Mahima Gadiyalu, reaches countless homes, sharing the message of hope, healing, and salvation.

Through his leadership, Messiah Fellowship has grown into a thriving community of believers in Vijayawada. The Messiah Convention Center stands as a testimony to God’s faithfulness and serves as a place where the Word of God continues to be proclaimed.

Pastor Eliazer and his wife, Mrs. Kamala, have been blessed with two sons and a daughter, as well as eight grandchildren. Their family remains deeply rooted in faith and ministry.
